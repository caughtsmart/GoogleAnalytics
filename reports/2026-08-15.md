## Flags & recommendations

1. **In-store events are working, and the data says lean into them.** A
   draft night plus two launches produced the best POS day on record and
   47 counter transactions. With TCG deliberately leaving the website, the
   events are what convert that demand — worth making sure the next MTG
   and Lorcana releases get the same treatment.
2. **Nothing here contradicts the wind-down.** TCG at 40% looks like a
   reversal but it is one launch weekend, and the bulk went through the
   till. Online TCG stayed modest. Judge the trend on ordinary days.
3. **The Topps blog spike is back — 329 sessions, still £0.** It never
   went away; it just fell out of view on quiet days. Over 10–14 Aug it has
   drawn roughly 1,100 sessions and produced no revenue at all. Worth
   deciding whether to monetise it (a football-cards range, or at minimum
   an internal link to relevant stock) or accept it as noise and exclude it
   from Organic Search readings.
4. **PMax sessions fell back to 228** from 631 — the two-day surge was
   delivery volatility, not a trend. No action.
