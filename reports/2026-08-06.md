## Flags & recommendations

1. **Bot mitigation is being evaded, not beaten.** Same 1280×1200 headless
   Chrome, but relocated to US/Singapore/HK/Japan/Germany with a UK
   foothold appearing. If the Cloudflare rule keys on country, it is losing
   ground by design. Action: switch the rule to match on behaviour or
   fingerprint (JA4/bot score, or challenge every non-UK *datacentre* ASN)
   rather than a country list, and review Cloudflare's own challenge
   pass-rate to see how many are solving it.
2. **The Gift Card campaign got opens but almost no clicks.** Sent 5 Aug
   14:23 to **30,270 recipients** (7× a normal send): 23.3% open rate
   (7,031 opens) but only **28 unique clicks** — 0.4% click-to-open against
   a 6–35% norm for this account. It earned £717.77 from 4 orders. Opens
   that big with clicks that small point at the creative or the CTA, not
   deliverability. Action: check the email renders a visible, tappable
   gift-card button on mobile before repeating the format.
3. **UTM fix is holding.** That campaign went out with
   `add_tracking_params: true` and a variation parameter — the first clean
   send since 29 July. Flow-level fixes (cross-sell, Abandoned Checkout
   Final, 10 Welcome variants, 6 SMS) still outstanding.
4. **Attribution lag confirmed for a third day.** Tuesday's 2,614
   Unassigned sessions restated overnight to 143, and 4 Aug's revenue
   firmed from £1,148 to £1,285.64 (24 orders). Yesterday's £321 in
   Unassigned will re-resolve — no action.
