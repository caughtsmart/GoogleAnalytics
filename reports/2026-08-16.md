   weekend traffic repeats — this is exactly the non-TCG demand the
   business is pivoting toward.

   its own, so still not a pattern, but worth watching into next week. If
   it persists on a normal trading day it is a funnel problem.

   Third consecutive day. The decision from Friday's report stands: either
