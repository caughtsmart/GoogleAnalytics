## Flags & recommendations

1. **The Ogor Mawtribes email was opened and then ignored: 27.7% opens,
   0.45% clicks, 2 orders, £47.56.** The targeting instinct was right —
   Ogor Mawtribes was flagged on 16 Aug as the week's quiet success — but a
   28% open rate against a 0.45% click rate is a content problem, not an
   audience problem. Over a thousand engaged Games Workshop customers
   opened it and did not click. **Worth reviewing what that email actually
   asked people to do** before the next range gets the same treatment.
2. **Check what the SMS send cost.** It delivered 5,849 messages and
   returned £217.62 — £0.037 per recipient. UK SMS typically runs
   £0.03–0.05 a message, so this send may have roughly broken even or lost
   money before any product cost. It drove clicks well (4.7%, ten times the
   email), so the list is responsive; the question is whether the
   economics work at full-list size rather than to a tighter segment.
3. **The day being soft is expected, not a problem.** 21% below the
   adjusted 7-day average, in the end-of-summer-holidays window recorded in
   the playbook yesterday. No funnel signal: cart-adds 69, checkouts 25,
   completed 21, all within the week's range.
4. **Online baskets recovered to £49.10 from £27.78.** That ends the
   two-day soft run I flagged, and lands above August's £47.71 average. The
   soft baskets were the quiet days, not a basket problem.
