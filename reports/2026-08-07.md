## Flags & recommendations

1. **GA4 is under-reporting revenue by roughly two-thirds, and it is not
   new.** On 6 Aug: GA4 16 orders/£575.11 against 23 Online Store orders
   /£1,688.07 in Shopify. A spot check of 22 July shows the same pattern
   (GA4 £2,364 vs £3,446 of web orders on the first page alone), so this is
   a long-standing measurement gap, not a fresh break. The likely mix is
   consent-mode blocking, ad-blockers and iOS tracking prevention.
   **Action: treat GA4 as a traffic and behaviour tool, not a revenue source
   of truth — read revenue from Shopify.** Worth checking whether the
   Shopify–GA4 integration is running server-side; if not, enabling it would
   close much of the gap.
2. **Gift cards are invisible to GA4 entirely.** Two orders worth £750
   produced zero item-level purchase events despite 15 add-to-carts. Judge
   the gift-card campaign on Shopify numbers; on those it is working.
3. **Recent "soft trading" calls were overstated.** The last three reports
   read revenue straight from GA4 and flagged a decline. Against Shopify,
   6 Aug was a normal day across four sales channels. The *direction* of
   this week's reporting still stands (paid channels are quiet, AOV is
   lower) but the severity does not.
4. **Bot flood steady at ~4,000/day.** Same fingerprint, still rotating
   countries. The geo-based Cloudflare rule is not going to catch this —
   a behaviour or bot-score rule is the durable fix.
