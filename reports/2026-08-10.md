## Flags & recommendations

1. **The Summer Sale send worked on traffic and carts.** Email sessions
   went 84 → 321 and SMS 1 → 61; add-to-carts more than doubled (141 →
   321) and checkouts rose 25 → 34. Online orders hit the week's high.
2. **But Klaviyo is crediting the send with only £348.85 from 10 orders,
   against 44 online orders worth £2,157 on the day.** The email pulled 383
   unique clicks and the SMS another 54 — 437 clicks producing 10
   attributed orders is not credible next to the day's actual sales. Most
   likely the attribution window or the click-tracking on the sale links is
   under-counting. Action: check the campaign's attribution settings before
   judging the sale a disappointment; on Shopify's numbers it was the best
   online day of the week.
3. **The SMS earned an 11.8% click rate on 456 recipients** — far above the
   email's 2.2% — yet logged zero conversions, which reinforces flag 2.
   SMS is worth more attention as a channel regardless of the attribution
   question.
4. **TCG at 14% of gross.** Wind-down continues ahead of expectation, and
   the non-TCG mix broadened into board games and RPG books rather than
   leaning entirely on Games Workshop. Nothing to action — noting progress.
