## Flags & recommendations

1. **Revenue is trending up three days running while traffic collapses.**
   That combination is only possible because the traffic that vanished was
   bots. Real visits have been steady at ~1,130–1,360 throughout, so
   nothing about actual demand has changed — the numbers just got honest.
2. **Baskets are holding above £70.** Third day at that level after a week
   at £47–50. With TCG at 13% and hobby/collectible lines filling the gap,
   the mix shift is producing better baskets, not just fewer orders.
3. **Organic Search doubled to 459 sessions.** Worth watching for a couple
   of days: it may simply be real organic traffic becoming visible now the
   bot noise has gone, rather than genuine growth. Do not act on it yet.
4. **Nothing needs attention today.** No campaigns sent, no dead pages, no
   anomalies beyond the bot decline, which is good news.
