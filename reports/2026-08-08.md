## Flags & recommendations

1. **Baskets got smaller and fewer people started one.** Add-to-carts
   halved (222 → 121) and checkouts halved (38 → 19) versus Thursday, on
   flat real traffic. One quiet Friday is not a trend, but if carts stay
   this low into next week it is the first thing to dig into.
2. **The month-over-month decline is mostly the TCG wind-down.** *(Revised
   later on 8 Aug after Graham confirmed TCG is being removed from the site.)*
   Gross sales fell from £4,109/day to £2,581/day across the two halves of
   the window — **down 37%** — but tagged TCG fell 66% (£1,538 → £518/day)
   against non-TCG's 20% (£2,571 → £2,063/day), so **TCG is at least 67% of
   the decline**. The early-July £6–8k days were TCG events: 14 Jul was 78%
   TCG, 16 Jul 83%. The continuing business is softer, not collapsing.
3. **The gift card promotion keeps earning.** Another £500 yesterday, on
   top of £750 on 6 Aug — **£1,250 in three days** from a campaign GA4
   reported as producing nothing. Worth a follow-up send to the openers who
   did not click, given clicks were the weak point.
4. **Prismatic Evolutions Super Premium**: 188 views in 7 days online with
   one unit and no online sale, while a "Prismatic SPC" sold in-store at
   £199.99. Worth confirming those are the same item — if so, the online
   listing is drawing traffic to stock that has just left the building.
