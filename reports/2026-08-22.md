## Flags & recommendations

1. **Confirm the £5,525 of exchange-line stock.** Nine SKUs and £2,420 in a
   single day is a different animal from Tuesday's single swap. Most likely
   a planned bulk trade-out of Pokémon wind-down stock — which would be good
   news and worth saying out loud — but it needs confirming, because the
   alternative is £5,525 of inventory leaving in four days with no order
   records behind it.
2. **The tighter segment is the lesson from the Paint Rack email — apply it
   to the next send.** Cutting 1,089 unengaged recipients raised the open
   rate 7.6 points and quadrupled revenue per recipient. Yesterday's AK
   Interactive sales (£101.29) landed the day after it went out. The click
   rate is still low at 0.69%, so the creative has further to go, but the
   audience question looks answered.
3. **Nothing here needs fixing — this was the strongest day of the run.**
   Record order count, record online orders, best conversion rate, baskets
   at £52.16 for a third day above £50. Worth noting how much of it was
   Games Workshop rather than TCG: the pivot is carrying the business.
4. **Watch the bot count on Monday.** 79 → 454 in a day on the same
   fingerprint, with Direct rising in step. Under threshold and not worth
   acting on yet, but the last rebound started exactly this way.
