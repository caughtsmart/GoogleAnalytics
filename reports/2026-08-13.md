   counter. The POS-only policy is doing exactly what it was meant to.
2. **Cross-network (PMax) sessions doubled to 559**, now the third-largest
   channel. Worth a look at the Google Ads side to see whether that is a
   budget or bidding change, and whether it is paying for itself — the
   sibling Ads routine will have the spend figures.
3. **Checkouts fell to 27 from 43** on similar add-to-carts (166 vs 199).
   One day, and revenue held, so not yet a concern — but if checkouts stay
   soft while carts hold, that is a funnel problem worth chasing.
4. **Organic Search normalised to 136** as expected once the Topps blog
   spike passed, confirming yesterday's read that it was a one-off with no
   commercial value.
