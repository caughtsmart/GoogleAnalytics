## Flags & recommendations

1. **The bot mitigation is losing ground again.** From 274 to 1,487 in
   three days on the same fingerprint and the same country spread. If the
   Cloudflare rule was tightened around 10 Aug, that tightening is being
   worked around. Worth checking whether the rule is still active and
   whether Cloudflare's challenge pass-rate has moved.
2. **The week is genuinely better: +16% revenue, +13% orders.** That is the
   number to hold on to. It survived a launch weekend at one end and a soft
   Sunday at the other, and it happened while TCG shrank — so the non-TCG
   pivot is carrying real weight now.
3. **eBay had its best day of the run (19 orders, £396).** It has been
   quietly consistent all week. Given TCG is leaving the website, worth
   asking whether eBay deserves more attention as a clearance channel.
4. **Baskets softened to £45.23 online** from £66 on Saturday. One quiet
   Sunday, and checkouts recovered to 41 from 28, so no action — but it
   ends the run of £65–70 days.
